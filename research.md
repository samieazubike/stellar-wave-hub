## Problem Addressed

Liquidity is essential for any decentralized exchange to function effectively. On the Stellar Decentralized Exchange (DEX), some trading pairs historically suffered from low liquidity and high slippage, making it difficult for users to execute large trades efficiently. There was no robust incentive mechanism for market makers to provide liquidity to the markets that needed it most.

## How Aquarius Uses Stellar and Technical Approach

Aquarius solves this by introducing a decentralized liquidity management protocol for the Stellar network. It leverages the Stellar DEX and the native AQUA token to incentivize liquidity provision. 

The technical approach revolves around a system of "bribes" and rewards. Users who provide liquidity to selected pairs on the Stellar DEX receive AQUA rewards. The protocol uses Stellar's unique architecture—specifically, its built-in order books and automated market maker (AMM) functionality—to track and distribute these rewards.

Community members use AQUA tokens to vote on which market pairs should receive liquidity rewards, effectively directing incentives to the most vital parts of the ecosystem. This governance mechanism is facilitated through Stellar's trustlines and transaction features, ensuring a decentralized and transparent process. Aquarius is closely integrated with major Stellar wallets like LOBSTR and interfaces like StellarX, utilizing Stellar SEP standards to create a seamless user experience.
