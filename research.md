## Why This Matches the Task

Beans App is a prominent project within the Stellar ecosystem and the Stellar Wave Program. It aims to solve real-world problems regarding financial inclusion and cross-border payments. It demonstrates strong use of the Stellar network for fast, low-cost settlements, and integrates with Soroban-based DeFi protocols for its yield features.

## Technical Architecture

![Beans App Interface](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/642fb32de87a48d3c780840d/645d221d5b096d72eb68d2b0_Mockup-3.webp)

Beans is a non-custodial wallet built on the Stellar blockchain designed to make cross-border payments and digital savings highly accessible to the general public, regardless of their cryptocurrency knowledge. The app was created to tackle major barriers to global financial inclusion, such as the high costs and slow speeds associated with traditional cross-border transactions, as well as the complexity of standard crypto interfaces. By abstracting away the technical complexities, Beans provides a user-friendly experience similar to popular payment apps like Venmo or CashApp, but with the added benefits of blockchain technology.

The project solves financial exclusion and the inefficiencies of traditional banking. For the billions of people who lack access to formal banking, Beans offers a digital-first alternative that allows them to store and move money without needing a traditional bank account. Additionally, in regions suffering from high inflation, Beans enables users to convert volatile local currencies into stablecoins, preserving their purchasing power. 

From a technical perspective, Beans leverages the Stellar network for its low-cost, high-speed transactions. It integrates with Stellar’s SEP-24 protocol to connect with "anchors" like MoneyGram, facilitating seamless on-ramps and off-ramps between digital assets and local fiat cash. The architecture is strictly non-custodial, meaning users retain full control of their funds with private keys encrypted and stored locally on the user's device. For its yield-generating "Earn" features, Beans integrates Soroban smart contracts from decentralized lending protocols like Blend.Capital to automate yield generation. Beans was founded by Wouter Arkink and is supported by the Stellar Community Fund.
