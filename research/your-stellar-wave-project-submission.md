## Description
KindFi+ is a decentralized fundraising and social impact platform built on the Stellar network. It connects global donors with grassroots projects in developing regions through a transparent, low-cost, and accessible funding channel. Traditional crowdfunding platforms often impose high fees, require bank accounts, and lack donor accountability, preventing many high-impact initiatives from receiving support. KindFi+ solves this problem by leveraging Stellar's instant, near-zero-cost transactions and decentralized infrastructure.

The platform supports both fiat and crypto contributions, using Stellar anchors to enable on/off ramps for local currencies. Donors can give with a mobile wallet or a bank card, while project creators receive funds directly to their Stellar account. Every transaction is recorded on the public ledger, creating an immutable and auditable trail. To further enhance trust, KindFi+ uses Soroban smart contracts to automate escrow and milestone-based payouts. These contracts release funds only when verifiable conditions are met, such as submission of progress reports or achievement of social impact metrics.

KindFi+ also includes a governance token that allows community members to vote on allocation of pooled donations and review project proposals. This participatory approach increases transparency and community alignment. The project is designed to be open source and collaborates with local organizations to ensure real-world impact. It also provides dashboards and analytics by indexing Stellar transaction history, enabling donors to see exactly how funds are used. The combination of Stellar's speed and low cost with Soroban's programmability makes KindFi+ a practical solution for global social impact fundraising.

## Problem Solved
Traditional crowdfunding platforms are expensive, opaque, and inaccessible to many communities globally. They charge high transaction fees, require banking infrastructure, and provide limited accountability for how funds are used. This prevents small-scale, high-impact projects from getting the support they need. KindFi+ addresses these issues by offering an open, low-cost, and transparent funding mechanism powered by Stellar.

## How It Uses Stellar
- Uses Stellar for ultra-low-cost, fast cross-border transactions.
- Leverages Stellar's public ledger for full transparency of donations.
- Integrates Stellar anchors to convert fiat to crypto and vice versa.
- Deploys Soroban smart contracts for escrow and milestone-based releases.
- Utilizes Stellar's asset issuance and decentralized exchange for project tokens.

## Technical Approach
- Frontend: Web app with React and Stellar-based wallet connections.
- Backend: Node.js services interacting with Stellar Horizon API.
- Smart Contracts: Rust-based Soroban contracts for escrow, voting, and token management.
- Data: Indexes Stellar transaction history for dashboards and analytics.
- Security: Multi-sig accounts on Stellar for key operations.
