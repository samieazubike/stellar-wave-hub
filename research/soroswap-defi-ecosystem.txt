SOROSWAP IN THE STELLAR DEFI ECOSYSTEM
========================================

This document provides context on Soroswap's role in the Stellar DeFi ecosystem.

STELLAR DEFI LANDSCAPE (2026):
-------------------------------

Total Value Locked (TVL) Growth:
- Stellar DeFi TVL reached ~$200M in April 2026
- Significant growth driven by RWA tokenization and DeFi protocols

Major DeFi Protocols on Stellar:

1. BLEND (Lending Protocol)
   - TVL: $80M+ (Q1 2026)
   - Lending and borrowing primitive
   - Integration with other protocols

2. AQUARIUS (DEX)
   - TVL: $40M+ (Jan-Feb 2026)
   - Independent decentralized exchange
   - Reliable liquidity hub

3. SOROSWAP.FINANCE (DEX + Aggregator)
   - First DEX aggregator on Stellar
   - Aggregates liquidity from all Stellar DEXs
   - Developed by PaltaLabs
   - Part of Soroban Adoption Fund ($100M program)

4. DEFINDEX
   - DeFi index and vault protocol
   - Integrates with Blend pools
   - Makes DeFi access easier for dApps

5. PHOENIX DEX
   - Soroban-based AMM
   - Integrated into Soroswap aggregator

SOROSWAP'S UNIQUE POSITION:
----------------------------

As the FIRST DEX AGGREGATOR on Stellar, Soroswap:
- Connects fragmented liquidity across multiple DEXs
- Provides best execution prices for traders
- Serves as infrastructure for other applications
- Enables composability in Stellar DeFi

STELLAR DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION SUPPORT:
---------------------------------------

Soroban Adoption Fund:
- $100M allocated to Soroban projects
- Soroswap officially recognized in SDF press release (March 2024)
- Listed alongside Axelar, Allbridge, Band Protocol
- Received funding during Soroban testnet phase

Stellar Community Fund (SCF):
- Additional funding program for Stellar projects
- PaltaLabs/Soroswap participated in SCF rounds
- Over $10M distributed to 130+ projects

ECOSYSTEM IMPACT:
-----------------

1. Liquidity Aggregation
   - Unifies liquidity from Soroban DEXs and Classic DEX
   - Reduces slippage for traders
   - Improves capital efficiency

2. Developer Infrastructure
   - Soroswap API enables wallet integrations
   - soroban-react framework for dApp development
   - Design patterns for other DeFi protocols

3. DeFi Composability
   - Other protocols can build on Soroswap
   - Pricing oracles for DeFi applications
   - Swap functionality as a service

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES:
-----------------------

- Access to more liquidity sources
- Optimal routing algorithms

vs. Other Blockchains:
