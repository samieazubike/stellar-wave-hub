## Team and Community Research (Comprehensive)

### Team Background
Decaf was co-founded by **Rick Martin** (CEO) and **Fernanda Orduña Rangel** (COO). Rick brings extensive experience in software engineering and product leadership, with a vision to simplify blockchain for non-technical users. Fernanda leads operations and market expansion, leveraging her deep understanding of the Latin American financial landscape to drive adoption in high-need corridors like Colombia and Venezuela. The team has consistently been recognized by the Stellar Development Foundation (SDF) for their proactive involvement in ecosystem governance and humanitarian efforts.

### Community Adoption and Metrics
Decaf has successfully scaled into a global platform, most notably in Latin America where hyperinflation and lack of traditional banking create a massive demand for stable digital dollars.
- **User Base:** Thousands of active users, primarily in Colombia, Mexico, and Venezuela, using the wallet for daily payments and savings.
- **Integrations:** Decaf is a primary wallet partner for MoneyGram’s Stellar-based cash-to-fiat service, enabling users in over 184 countries to access physical cash at thousands of locations.
- **Humanitarian Impact:** The platform is a critical partner in the UNHCR (United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees) Stellar-based aid disbursement project, ensuring transparent and instant delivery of aid to refugees in regions such as Ukraine and beyond.

### Ecosystem Partnerships
- **MoneyGram:** Strategic partnership for physical on/off-ramps.
- **Stellar Development Foundation (SDF):** Repeated recipient of grants and featured participant in the Stellar Community Fund.
- **Drips Network:** Active participant in the Stellar Wave contribution cycles, providing feedback and open-source contributions to the ecosystem tooling.

## Real-World Impact (Beyond Technicals)

Decaf’s impact is measurable in human terms. In remote areas of Colombia, where banks are non-existent and cash transit is dangerous, entrepreneurs use Decaf to pay employees in USDC securely. Employees then leverage MoneyGram to convert only what they need into local pesos. This "hyper-local" utility solves real-world security and liquidity problems that traditional banking systems have failed to address for decades. By focusing on the "people" rather than just the "code," Decaf has become a symbol of how the Stellar Wave Program drives meaningful change.
