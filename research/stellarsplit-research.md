Project Name: StellarSplit
Category: Payments / Social Finance
Description: StellarSplit is a decentralized application (dApp) designed to revolutionize the social payment experience by facilitating seamless bill-splitting and group expense management on the Stellar network. Developed with the goal of eliminating the complexities of traditional fiat-based settlement platforms (such as high fees, cross-border delays, and centralized custody), StellarSplit leverages Stellar's native capabilities for near-instant, low-cost asset transfers. The project provides a user-friendly interface that allows multiple parties to coordinate expenses, settle debts, and manage shared funds without relying on a central intermediary.

A critical component of StellarSplit’s current development is its integration with Soroban, Stellar's smart contract environment, to handle more sophisticated financial workflows. By implementing modular logic such as "Split Entities" and "Escrow Modules," the platform can enforce immutable rules for fund distribution—such as equal splits, weighted contributions based on custom ratios, or time-locked settlements. This smart-contract-driven approach provides a high degree of transparency and trust, as all settlement events and distribution rules are verifiable on the Stellar ledger. StellarSplit is a key participant in the Stellar Wave Program, utilizing recurring development cycles to enhance its on-chain coordination features, improve security through non-custodial wallet integrations (like Freighter), and build out a robust API for third-party developers. As a social finance infrastructure piece, it aims to empower communities with decentralized tools for collaborative financial management, reinforcing the Stellar network's utility as a leading platform for real-world payment solutions.
