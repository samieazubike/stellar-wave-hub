## Technical Architecture & Description

Beans App is a highly intuitive, non-custodial digital wallet built on the Stellar network. Its primary mission is to simplify cross-border payments, currency exchange, and yield generation for individuals who may not be familiar with cryptocurrency. By abstracting the complexities of blockchain technology, Beans provides a seamless experience akin to traditional fiat payment applications like Venmo or CashApp, but with the borderless and low-cost benefits of Stellar. Because it operates on Stellar, transactions settle in seconds with near-zero fees.

Traditional cross-border payments are plagued by high fees, slow settlement times, and opaque exchange rates. While crypto solves many of these issues, it introduces steep learning curves—such as seed phrase management and gas fees—that alienate non-technical users. Beans App solves both problems simultaneously: it eliminates the high fees and delays of traditional remittance while completely hiding the technical complexities of crypto.

Beans leverages the Stellar Horizon API and Stellar SDK to sign and submit transactions efficiently. It also integrates a unique decentralized account recovery system in partnership with Public Node. This replaces the intimidating requirement of managing private keys or 12-word seed phrases with a familiar email and PIN combination. This drastically lowers the barrier to entry for mainstream users while maintaining the security benefits of a non-custodial architecture. Furthermore, the wallet also integrates deeply with the Stellar Disbursement Platform (SDP) to act as a seamless endpoint for bulk payouts, and utilizes MoneyGram Access to provide widespread cash on-ramps and off-ramps globally.
